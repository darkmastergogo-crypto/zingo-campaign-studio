Your real workflow

From creative to controlled delivery.

The interface follows the same sequence your automation uses: upload media once, load eligible recipients, validate each recipient, check suppression and duplicates, then call Meta.

Operational truth, not vanity metrics.

Campaigns are marked accepted only after the production webhook responds successfully. Delivery records remain in your Google Sheet Message_Log.
